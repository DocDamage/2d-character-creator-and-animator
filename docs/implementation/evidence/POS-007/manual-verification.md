# Manual Verification Scenario

1. Bind a rig and enter a new pose ID/name in **Saved Poses**.
2. Draw a multi-point gesture in **Sketch-to-Pose Suggestion** and choose
   **Generate Suggested Pose**.
3. Confirm the generated pose appears in the saved list and its thumbnail can
   be reviewed before separately applying it.
4. Confirm the bound rig did not change when the suggestion was generated.
5. Clear the sketch or supply fewer than two points and confirm the panel
   reports a recoverable error without creating a pose.
